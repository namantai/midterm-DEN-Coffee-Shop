About

DEN COFFEE is a small coffee shop focused on good coffee and a friendly atmosphere.

Our goal is to create a comfortable place where customers can enjoy coffee, study, work, or spend time with friends.

Before your visit

Which drinks come without milk?

Espresso and Americano are our black coffees. An espresso is a 30 ml shot. For a longer drink, try the 250 ml Americano.

Do you serve cold coffee?

Yes. Our iced latte comes in a 400 ml glass. See the drink menu for sizes and prices.

Can I come here to study?

Yes. You are welcome to study, work or meet friends here. Take a look around in our photo gallery.
